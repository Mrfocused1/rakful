Rakful Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Summary

Rakful analyses the photos you allow on your iPhone and keeps what it learns on your iPhone. You don't need an account, and we don't track you, show ads or sell data.

  • Photo analysis runs on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Your photos are never uploaded unless you turn on an optional online feature and agree to it first.
  • Everything Rakful stores (items, listings, history, settings) lives on your iPhone and is not synced to iCloud.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete any or all of your Rakful data in Settings at any time. Rakful never edits or deletes your photos.

This policy applies to the Rakful app for iPhone.

Who we are

Rakful is published by Meridian Studio Group ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for any personal information processed through the app's online features.

For privacy questions or requests, email us at [privacy contact email]. We aim to reply within one month.

Your photos

Rakful reads only the photos you give it access to, and analyses them on your iPhone. You choose full or limited access in iOS, and can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

  • What the analysis does: Apple's Vision framework recognises the type of object in a photo and any visible text, such as a brand name on a label. This runs on your device; no image leaves it.
  • What we store: the photo's iOS identifier and creation date, a numeric image fingerprint used to group photos of the same item and skip duplicates, and the suggested category, brand and confidence. We do not store copies of your library.
  • What we never do: edit, move or delete your photos, or upload them without your separate permission (see Optional online features).

If you take or import a photo for a listing, Rakful saves a downsized copy (at most 2048 pixels on the longest side) inside the app so it can be used in that listing.

All of this stays on your iPhone, in Rakful's private storage. It is not synced to iCloud and we cannot see it.

What we don't do

Rakful contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third-party software libraries.

  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app never asks for permission to.
  • We don't build advertising profiles or show ads.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't require you to create an account or give us your name, email or phone number.

Apple services the app uses

These features run through Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Subscriptions: Rakful Plus and Pro are bought, billed and managed through the App Store. Apple tells the app which plan is active; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.
  • Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 and later): if you turn it on, listing text is written by Apple's on-device model from the details of that item. You can turn it off in Rakful's Settings.
  • Notifications: scan results, the morning report and listing reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. You choose which ones you get.
  • Charge & Discover: with Plus or Pro, iOS may run photo analysis in the background while your iPhone is charging. It is the same on-device analysis described above.

Optional online features

Two features send information off your iPhone, and both are off until you choose to use them. Where a feature isn't offered in your version of the app, nothing is sent.

Connecting eBay. You sign in on eBay's own page; we never see your eBay password. eBay then shows you the permissions Rakful is asking for, and you can decline.

  • We store the access tokens eBay issues, encrypted on our server, and eBay's anonymous ID for your account. We don't store your eBay username.
  • When you publish a listing, we send its text, price, condition, photos and item location to eBay.
  • To help you ship a sold item, we fetch the order from eBay, including the delivery address and ship-by date, and send any tracking number you enter back to eBay.
  • eBay's use of this information is covered by eBay's User Privacy Notice.

Sold prices. Rakful doesn't estimate prices. "See Sold Prices on eBay" opens eBay's website with a search for the item's name, brand and model, and nothing else is sent.

Cloud identification. If you turn this on and agree in the app, Rakful sends one downsized photo of an item to our server to suggest its brand and model. The photo is used only to return that suggestion and is not kept afterwards.

Our server is run for us by a cloud hosting provider that processes data only on our instructions. If data is processed outside the UK, we use the safeguards UK law requires, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement.

Keeping and deleting your information

You control how long Rakful keeps your data, and you can delete it yourself at any time in Settings.

Control in SettingsWhat it does
Keep Activity HistoryKeeps history for 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or until you delete it
Delete Discovery HistoryRemoves scan history and activity
Remove Imported ImagesDeletes photos you took or imported for listings
Revoke IntegrationsDisconnects marketplaces and removes their tokens from your iPhone
Delete All DataErases everything Rakful has stored

Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. None of these controls touch your photo library.

If you connected eBay, disconnecting or deleting all data also deletes your eBay tokens and order information from our server. We also delete it when eBay tells us your eBay account has been closed. Server data is otherwise kept only while your eBay account stays connected.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send it to you or another service in a portable format

Most of your data never reaches us, so the in-app controls above are the quickest way to use these rights. For anything on our server, email us at the address in Who we are.

Legal bases. We process data for an online feature because you asked us to provide it (contract). We rely on your consent for cloud identification, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children, security and changes

Children. Rakful is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Security. Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS encryption and the app's private storage, and marketplace tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only. Anything sent to our server or to eBay travels over encrypted HTTPS.

Changes. If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is shared, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
